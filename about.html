About Asaase Kitchen

Asaase means earth — food that comes from the ground up.

A modern Ghanaian restaurant inside JP Sakaman Service Station, built on fair prices, honest service, and a kitchen that shows up the same way every day.

Our story

Started with a simple
idea: cook it right.

4.6★ from 650 Google reviews — earned one plate at a time.

Asaase Kitchen — Sakaman opened with one goal: serve real Ghanaian food, cooked properly, at a price that makes sense for everyday people — not just special occasions. That meant a clean, comfortable dining room instead of a roadside stall, a menu built around dishes people actually grew up eating, and a kitchen that treats every plate — dine-in, takeaway, or delivery — the same way.

We're inside JP Sakaman Service Station, which makes us an easy stop whether you're filling up on fuel or specifically coming for the fufu. Customers tell us the same things again and again: friendly staff, a clean space, generous portions, and food that tastes like it should.
