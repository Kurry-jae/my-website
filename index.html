About the kitchen

Home cooking, sized for
a proper Accra appetite.

Asaase means "earth" — food that comes from the ground up, cooked the way it's meant to be.

Asaase Kitchen — Sakaman is a modern Ghanaian restaurant serving authentic local cuisine in a clean, comfortable dining room inside JP Sakaman Service Station. We keep the menu generous, the prices fair, and the kitchen consistent — which is why customers keep coming back.
