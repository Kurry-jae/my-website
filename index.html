About the kitchen

Home cooking, sized for
a proper Accra appetite.

Asaase means "earth" — food that comes from the ground up, cooked the way it's meant to be.

Asaase Kitchen — Sakaman is a modern Ghanaian restaurant serving authentic local cuisine in a clean, comfortable dining room inside JP Sakaman Service Station. We keep the menu generous, the prices fair, and the kitchen consistent — which is why customers keep coming back, whether they're sitting down for lunch or picking up dinner on the way home.

We read every review. Where we've fallen short — a portion that felt small, a price that changed, a queue that ran long — we've said so honestly and worked to fix it. Good food and honest service are the two things we don't compromise on.
